# Numerical results

**ACO–SVR wearable textile antenna study · IEEE GCITC 2023**

The code release includes 12 regression approaches evaluated under two configurations: five inputs including operating frequency, and four geometric inputs without operating frequency.

## Recomputable table results

`results/table_scores.csv` contains 24 rows and four calculated operators per row. All **96 printed table entries** are produced at the manuscript's reported two-decimal precision by the saved numerical evaluation system. The observed inputs, estimated signal-strength vectors, kernel coefficients and scoring measures are retained in machine-readable form.

## Numerical and simulation products

- **30,384** full-population forward glucose-analogue signal-strength predictions (24 × 1,266) in `results/forward_predictions.csv`.
- **21 ants × 240 rounds** of ant-colony exploration, with the selected search parameters and convergence trace in `results/`.
- **Eight physical geometries**, including patch and slot lengths and widths, represented directly by the source dataset.
- **Four cited substrate simulations**, cotton (with and without body tissue), polyester and poly-amide, with reported gains of **6.8, 9.6, 4.7 and 5.9 dBi**.
- **Four cited ACO–SVM/HFSS comparisons**: cotton 10 / 9.6 dBi; polyester 4.6 / 4.7 dBi; poly-amide 6.2 / 5.9 dBi; S11 -19.7 / -20 dB.

The latter antenna gain and S11 results come from the published HFSS tables and are not independently regenerated by a full-wave simulator in this software release.

## Figures and website

Figures under `figures/` show actual source frequency sweeps, the ACO optimization objective and HFSS design values. The GitHub Pages site in `docs/index.html` offers interactive exploration of the underlying saved predictions, evaluation tables and geometry designs.
