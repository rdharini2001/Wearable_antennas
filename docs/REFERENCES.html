Methodological Literature

The following references support the computational components of the textile antenna companion study. The literature does not certify the fitted reproduction scores as independent performance estimates.

  1. Raghavan, D., Sujatha, B., Chinmayi, B. S., Rao, B. B., & Kiran, J. (2023). Ant Colony Optimization Based Support Vector Regression Algorithm for Performance Prediction of Wearable Textile Antennas. IEEE GCITC. DOI. — Original experiment and all reported numerical endpoints, substrate values and patch equations.

  2. Dorigo, M., & Blum, C. (2005). Ant colony optimization theory: A survey. Theoretical Computer Science, 344, 243–278. DOI. — Pheromone-weighted discrete search and its relationship to combinatorial optimization.

  3. Cortes, C., & Vapnik, V. (1995). Support-vector networks. Machine Learning, 20, 273–297. DOI. — Kernelized support-vector learning and the rationale behind SVR's kernel-based model family.

  4. Drucker, H., Burges, C. J. C., Kaufman, L., Smola, A., & Vapnik, V. (1997). Support vector regression machines. Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 9, 155–161. Proceedings. — Regression-specific support vector objective and ε-insensitive error tolerance.

  5. Kennedy, M. C., & O'Hagan, A. (2001). Bayesian calibration of computer models. Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 63, 425–464. DOI. — Formal motivation for separating the base computer model from a fitted discrepancy correction. WEAVE's correction is deterministic rather than a Bayesian posterior model.

  6. Brynjarsdóttir, J., & O'Hagan, A. (2014). Learning about physical parameters: The importance of model discrepancy. Inverse Problems, 30, 114007. DOI. — Explains why fitted parameters and discrepancies require careful identifiability interpretation.

  7. Hodson, T. O. (2022). Root-mean-square error (RMSE) or mean absolute error (MAE): when to use them or not. Geoscientific Model Development, 15, 5481–5487. DOI. — Correct definitions of predictive error metrics and the need for explicit evaluation populations.

  8. Afridi, M. A. (2015). Microstrip Patch Antenna Designing at 2.4 GHz Frequency. Biological and Chemical Research, 128–132. — Classical rectangular patch width, effective dielectric, fringing and length relationships; the original paper cites this as its reference [24].

Numerical source data: MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML, publication reference [16], retrieved as a validated copy of the 1,266 source numerical records.
