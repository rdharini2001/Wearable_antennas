# Methodological Literature

The following references support the computational components of the textile antenna companion study. The literature **does not** certify the fitted reproduction scores as independent performance estimates.

1. **Raghavan, D., Sujatha, B., Chinmayi, B. S., Rao, B. B., & Kiran, J. (2023).** *Ant Colony Optimization Based Support Vector Regression Algorithm for Performance Prediction of Wearable Textile Antennas.* IEEE GCITC. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/GCITC60406.2023.10425975). — Original experiment and all reported numerical endpoints, substrate values and patch equations.

2. **Dorigo, M., & Blum, C. (2005).** Ant colony optimization theory: A survey. *Theoretical Computer Science, 344*, 243–278. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2005.05.020). — Pheromone-weighted discrete search and its relationship to combinatorial optimization.

3. **Cortes, C., & Vapnik, V. (1995).** Support-vector networks. *Machine Learning, 20*, 273–297. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF00994018). — Kernelized support-vector learning and the rationale behind SVR's kernel-based model family.

4. **Drucker, H., Burges, C. J. C., Kaufman, L., Smola, A., & Vapnik, V. (1997).** Support vector regression machines. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems 9*, 155–161. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/1996/hash/d38901788c533e8286cb6400b40b386d-Abstract.html). — Regression-specific support vector objective and ε-insensitive error tolerance.

5. **Kennedy, M. C., & O'Hagan, A. (2001).** Bayesian calibration of computer models. *Journal of the Royal Statistical Society: Series B, 63*, 425–464. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/1467-9868.00294). — Formal motivation for separating the base computer model from a fitted discrepancy correction. WEAVE's correction is deterministic rather than a Bayesian posterior model.

6. **Brynjarsdóttir, J., & O'Hagan, A. (2014).** Learning about physical parameters: The importance of model discrepancy. *Inverse Problems, 30*, 114007. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/0266-5611/30/11/114007). — Explains why fitted parameters and discrepancies require careful identifiability interpretation.

7. **Hodson, T. O. (2022).** Root-mean-square error (RMSE) or mean absolute error (MAE): when to use them or not. *Geoscientific Model Development, 15*, 5481–5487. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5194/gmd-15-5481-2022). — Correct definitions of predictive error metrics and the need for explicit evaluation populations.

8. **Afridi, M. A. (2015).** Microstrip Patch Antenna Designing at 2.4 GHz Frequency. *Biological and Chemical Research*, 128–132. — Classical rectangular patch width, effective dielectric, fringing and length relationships; the original paper cites this as its reference [24].

**Numerical source data:** [MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML](https://github.com/MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML), publication reference [16], retrieved as a validated copy of the 1,266 source numerical records.
