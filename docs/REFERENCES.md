# References

1. D. Raghavan et al. (2023). Ant Colony Optimization Based Support Vector Regression Algorithm for Performance Prediction of Wearable Textile Antennas. IEEE GCITC. [doi:10.1109/GCITC60406.2023.10425975](https://doi.org/10.1109/GCITC60406.2023.10425975).
2. K. Socha and M. Dorigo (2008). Ant colony optimization for continuous domains. *European Journal of Operational Research* 185(3), 1155–1173. [doi:10.1016/j.ejor.2006.06.046](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejor.2006.06.046). Justification for continuous-domain ACO hyperparameter search.
3. A. J. Smola and B. Schölkopf (2004). A tutorial on support vector regression. *Statistics and Computing* 14, 199–222. [doi:10.1023/B:STCO.0000035301.49549.88](https://doi.org/10.1023/B:STCO.0000035301.49549.88).
4. B. Gustavsen and A. Semlyen (1999). Rational approximation of frequency domain responses by vector fitting. *IEEE Transactions on Power Delivery* 14(3), 1052–1061. [doi:10.1109/61.772353](https://doi.org/10.1109/61.772353). Supports response-aware spectral modeling as an alternative to treating sweep points independently; not an implementation dependency.
5. H. Akima (1970). A new method of interpolation and smooth curve fitting based on local procedures. *Journal of the ACM* 17(4), 589–602. [doi:10.1145/321607.321609](https://doi.org/10.1145/321607.321609). Local cubic interpolation candidate.
6. F. N. Fritsch and R. E. Carlson (1980). Monotone piecewise cubic interpolation. *SIAM Journal on Numerical Analysis* 17(2), 238–246. [doi:10.1137/0717021](https://doi.org/10.1137/0717021). Shape-preserving interpolation candidate.
7. R. J. Hyndman and A. B. Koehler (2006). Another look at measures of forecast accuracy. *International Journal of Forecasting* 22(4), 679–688. [doi:10.1016/j.ijforecast.2006.03.001](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijforecast.2006.03.001). Motivation for transparent, consistent evaluation definitions across response sequences.
8. scikit-learn developers. Cross-validation with grouped samples. [Official documentation](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/cross_validation.html). Design-exclusive evaluation for unseen antenna geometries.
9. [MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML](https://github.com/MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML). Antenna observation dataset cited as reference [16] by the conference article.
10. T. Gneiting and A. E. Raftery (2007). Strictly proper scoring rules, prediction, and estimation. *Journal of the American Statistical Association* 102(477), 359–378. [doi:10.1198/016214506000001437](https://doi.org/10.1198/016214506000001437). Continuous ranked probability score and distributional evaluation.
11. Y. Romano, E. Patterson and E. J. Candès (2019). Conformalized quantile regression. *Advances in Neural Information Processing Systems* 32. [arxiv:1905.03222](https://arxiv.org/abs/1905.03222). Calibration of prediction intervals using disjoint data.
12. J. O. Ramsay and B. W. Silverman (2005). *Functional Data Analysis*, 2nd ed. Springer. [doi:10.1007/b98888](https://doi.org/10.1007/b98888). Frequency sweeps as functional responses.
13. G. Peyré and M. Cuturi (2019). *Computational Optimal Transport*. Foundations and Trends in Machine Learning 11(5–6), 355–602. [doi:10.1561/2200000073](https://doi.org/10.1561/2200000073). Mathematical basis for one-dimensional optimal rank coupling and Wasserstein distribution distances.
14. R. Koenker and G. Bassett (1978). Regression quantiles. *Econometrica* 46(1), 33–50. [JSTOR 1913643](https://www.jstor.org/stable/1913643). Foundational literature on conditional quantiles and absolute loss.
