# Antenna data

`comm_antenna.csv` contains the operating frequency, patch and slot dimensions, and response strength for the 1,266 antenna observations used in the regression study. The source is the public antenna dataset referenced by the IEEE GCITC research article ([MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML](https://github.com/MHassaanButt/Antenna-design-using-ML)).

`substrate_simulation.csv` summarizes HFSS gain results for cotton, polyester and poly-amide substrates; `aco_hfss_comparison.csv` records gain and reflection-coefficient characterization reported in the study. Both electromagnetic tables retain their original quantities and units.
